# My Fix-It Helper

Simple step-by-step fixes for your home and car.

- 37 fixes: plumbing, doors and windows, walls and floors, electrical basics, appliances and heating/cooling, outdoor and garage, home safety and security, car basics.
- Every fix: safety first, tools, a parts list, the steps, and "call a pro if…".
- Store buttons on every part: Home Depot and Lowe's for home fixes; AutoZone, Advance Auto and O'Reilly for car fixes. They open the store's own search, so prices are always current.
- Shopping list and a seasonal home checklist (monthly, this season, yearly), saved on the phone.

Free: every fix, the shopping list and the checklist.
Premium (through CashCow, $1.99/mo, $14.99/yr or $29.99 once): My Home, with projects, a repair log with costs, and home reminders.
One CashCow account works here and in the other CashCow apps.

Fixes live in `fixes.js`. Stores and colors live in `brand.js`; after joining a store's affiliate
program, add the tracking link there and every button for that store earns.
Wording rule: general how-to information; safety first; call a pro for gas, the electrical panel, the roof, or anything unsure.

## Render (Static Site)
- Build command: `npm install && npm run build`
- Publish directory: `dist`
